Element diggs:ReferentType / diggs:name
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The name or abbreviation by which the referent is known, for example
GS (ground surface), KB (kelly bushing), RT (rotary table), SF (sea
floor) or TOC (top of core, or top of casing). Use the DIGGS
dictionary referentName.xml for the list of values; its entries
accept either the full name or a common abbreviation as the value.
Identify the entry with codeSpace, for example
referentName.xml#ground_surface; this also resolves an abbreviation
that two entries share.

Element diggs:ReferentType / diggs:referentType
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The kind of referent, which gives the reference point its physical
or logical meaning, for example ground surface, kelly bushing,
kickoff point, top of core or bottom of core. Values come from the
enumerated list, or "Other: xx", where xx is a user-defined term
whose meaning differs from every listed value (not a synonym of
one).

Element diggs:ReferentType / diggs:distanceAlong
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The position of the referent along the linear extent, as the
distance from its first coordinate in gml:posList. For example, a
kelly bushing at the first point has a distance of 0, and a ground
surface 5 ft below it has 5 ft.
