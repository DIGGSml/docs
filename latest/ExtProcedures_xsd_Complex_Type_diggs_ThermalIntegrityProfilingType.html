Element diggs:ThermalIntegrityProfilingType / diggs:cureTimeAtMeasurement
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The time elapsed since concrete placement when the reading was
taken. ASTM D7949 requires the measurement near the time of peak
temperature, which depends on element size and mix design; a reading
taken too early or too late understates the thermal contrast a
defect would produce.
