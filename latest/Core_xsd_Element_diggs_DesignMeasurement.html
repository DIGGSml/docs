Element diggs:DesignMeasurement
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
A design for a measurement: the planned counterpart of a realized
diggs:AbstractMeasurement. One type serves every kind of measurement, whether an
in-situ test, a laboratory test on a sample, a monitoring campaign, a measurement
taken while drilling or a geophysical survey, because what is plannable (what it is
done on, where, by what method, how often, how densely, for how long) is common to
all of them. A realized measurement cites its design through programDesignRef.
