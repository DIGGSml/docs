Element diggs:ResistivityReading
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
A single electrode reading within an in-situ resistivity test: the array geometry,
the resistance measured and the apparent resistivity computed from it. The
separation geometry is exactly one of three forms, selected by the procedure's
diggs:arrayType: a Wenner array gives diggs:electrodeSpacing alone, a Schlumberger
array the pair diggs:currentElectrodeSeparation and
diggs:potentialElectrodeSeparation, and a dipole-dipole array the pair
diggs:dipoleLength and diggs:nFactor. Independently of that form, a traverse also
gives diggs:readingLocation and an azimuthal survey diggs:orientation.
