Element diggs:CompositeAcceptanceCriterion
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
A requirement formed by combining two or more separately stated acceptance criteria
under one logical operator, for example the drilling refusal rule "penetration rate
below X and torque above Y". Each member is a normally declared criterion with its
own subject, statistic, operator, limit, basis and evaluated-against references,
evaluated on its own terms; the composite combines their verdicts and has no
statistic, basis or base length of its own. A member may have a different kind of
subject from the composite: a requirement about the act of construction, such as a
refusal rule, is judged on the construction activity cited through
constructionActivityRef, while the composite is judged on the feature. A
construction activity member applies to the feature named by that activity's
samplingFeatureRef; where several activities act on the feature, the latest by
activityDateTime counts. The composite substitutes
diggs:AbstractProgramSpecification, so it is accepted wherever a
diggs:PerformanceCriterion is.
