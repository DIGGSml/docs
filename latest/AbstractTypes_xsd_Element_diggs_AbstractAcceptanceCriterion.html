Element diggs:AbstractAcceptanceCriterion
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
Abstract head of the substitution group for acceptance criteria. A criterion states
one testable requirement: what is measured, how the measurements are reduced to one
value, the comparison applied and the limit compared against. As a program
specification it also carries the scope of the features it governs and may
specialize a more general criterion through basedOnRef. Its members differ in what
is measured: diggs:PerformanceCriterion measures a physical property of the
completed work, and diggs:DesignConformanceCriterion measures a geometric or
constructional property against its design intent. Each names its subject through
the parameterName of limit; use the DIGGS dictionary properties.xml for a
performance criterion and designProperty.xml for a design conformance criterion. A
specification that imposes several requirements on one property, such as a mean and
a minimum, is stated as several criteria that share a scope.
diggs:CompositeAcceptanceCriterion states the requirement that a logical combination
of other criteria holds; it is a program specification but does not substitute this
head, because it has no operator or limit of its own.
