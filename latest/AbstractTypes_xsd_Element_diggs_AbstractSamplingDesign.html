Element diggs:AbstractSamplingDesign
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
Abstract head of the substitution group for designs of samples and sampling
activities. Sibling of diggs:AbstractSamplingFeatureDesign, which covers designs of
sampling features. Its members include diggs:DesignSample, for a collected or
derived sample, and diggs:DesignGroutMix, a specialization of it for a manufactured
sample.
