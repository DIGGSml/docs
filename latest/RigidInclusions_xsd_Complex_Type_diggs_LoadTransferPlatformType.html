Element diggs:LoadTransferPlatformType / diggs:wpRef
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The one diggs:WorkingPlatform that this platform overlies. It is
mandatory so that wpElevation is accessible without spatially
associating the platform with the working platform, and not
repeatable because an LTP overlies exactly one working platform: two
platform levels would make the thickness of the LTP vary across its
extent, which the model does not allow. The target is one of the
diggs:WorkingPlatform objects held by
diggs:RITreatedArea/workingPlatform. Where a spatial test of the
extents disagrees with this reference, the reference governs. Given
by reference only; it is typed gml:ReferenceType rather than
diggs:FeatureReferenceType because diggs:WorkingPlatform is a
component object with a gml:id but no gml:identifier.

Element diggs:LoadTransferPlatformType / diggs:platformType

Annotations
 
The construction type of the load transfer platform, as built:
unreinforced granular fill, geogrid-reinforced fill, soil-mixed or
cemented raft, or (the extreme case) a structural concrete slab. It
is a construction-method classification, distinct from the lift
materialType, which remains a lithology and cannot say "structural
concrete slab". Corresponds to
diggs:RILTPSpecificationType/platformType on the design side. Values
come from the enumerated list, or "Other: xx", where xx is a
user-defined term whose meaning differs from every listed value (not
a synonym of one).

Element diggs:LoadTransferPlatformType / diggs:elevationTopLTP
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The elevation of the top of the load transfer platform. One LTP is
one zone with one top elevation across its whole extent, even where
that extent is a diggs:MultiSurface of several disjoint polygons
(surfaces that did not share the elevation would be separate
diggs:LoadTransferPlatform features), so this is a definite
position, not an averaged level. Where the featureExtent of the
platform is 3-D or compound, the geometry prevails; where it is 2-D
the geometry carries no elevation and this property is the
prevailing value, as for diggs:WorkingPlatformType/wpElevation. LTP
extents are commonly delivered as 2-D plan polygons, so this
property is usually the only carrier.

Element diggs:LoadTransferPlatformType / diggs:impliedLTPThickness
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The thickness of the load transfer platform as a whole, every lift
together, inferred as elevationTopLTP minus wpElevation. It is the
whole platform, not a lift, and it is not derived away when a
platform has a single lift: placing a lift needs
heightAboveWorkingPlatform and liftThickness, while describing the
platform needs elevationTopLTP and this total. It equals the
heightAboveWorkingPlatform of the topmost lift, and the sum of every
liftThickness when the lifts are contiguous. It is optional because
elevationTopLTP, which it is inferred from, is optional.

Element diggs:LoadTransferPlatformType / diggs:lifts
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The lifts laid down to form the LTP, each a discrete entity with a
known material and thickness. A platform has at least one lift.
Vertical invariant: for every lift, wpElevation plus
heightAboveWorkingPlatform must not exceed elevationTopLTP, and for
the topmost lift the two are equal, since its top is the top of the
platform; the equality is the checkable form. An LTP always has
thickness: elevationTopLTP is strictly greater than wpElevation, so
no lift may be of zero thickness. A zone with no load transfer
platform is recorded by omitting the diggs:LoadTransferPlatform
feature, not by declaring one of zero thickness; contrast
diggs:WorkingPlatform, where zero is meaningful (avgFillThickness is
zero where no fill was placed over natural ground). wpElevation is
that of the one working platform named by wpRef, where it is
mandatory and controlling.
