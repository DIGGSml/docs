Element diggs:AcceptanceCheck
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The verdict of evaluating one acceptance criterion against a subject: that the
verticality of a column was within tolerance, that a grout batch met its strength
requirement, that a test was performed to its procedure. Records a pass as well as a
fail; a failure that needs a decision is recorded as a diggs:NonConformance citing
this check. Contained in a diggs:AcceptanceEvaluation. The subject is identified by
at least one of samplingFeatureRef, sampleRef, samplingActivityRef,
constructionActivityRef, measurementRef or observationSystemRef, and subjectLocation
narrows it to the part concerned. gml:name carries a check reference where a project
numbers them; diggs:status is the state of this record (draft, reviewed, released),
which is distinct from outcome, the verdict on the work.
