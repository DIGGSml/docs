Element diggs:AbstractSamplingFeatureGroupType / diggs:extent
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The area over which this group acts, or within which its members
lie. An extent, not a reference point: a treated area, a monitoring
network or a paired investigation has no single meaningful position.
Optional, because a group need not be spatially delimited; a
borehole/CPT pair, for example, is defined by the proximity of its
two members. Two-dimensional; elevation is not meaningful for a
group extent. Where a concrete group also carries a scalar area, the
geometry prevails. May be given inline or by reference (xlink:href).
A group extent and a member's featureExtent are not the same thing
by default: a group's members often lie outside the area the group
is about, for example exploratory boreholes drilled beyond a treated
footprint. Where the two genuinely coincide, give the polygon once
and reference it from the other rather than writing the same
coordinates twice. See diggs:featureExtent.
