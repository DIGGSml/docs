Complex Type diggs:BoundingSurfacePropertyType
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
Property type that contains one object, which is a diggs:PlanarSurface, a
diggs:CompositeSurface, a diggs:MultiPlanarSurface or a diggs:TriangulatedSurface
inline, or refers to one with xlink:href. It holds one continuous bounding surface
of a diggs:SurfaceBoundedSolid: a diggs:PlanarSurface, diggs:CompositeSurface,
diggs:MultiPlanarSurface or diggs:TriangulatedSurface. It is narrower than
diggs:SurfacePropertyType, which also admits diggs:MultiSurface: a collection of
surfaces with no assumed adjacency cannot serve as one bounding surface, and a body
in disjoint pieces is expressed with a diggs:MultiSurface footprint instead. That
the members of a diggs:CompositeSurface, and the patches of a
diggs:MultiPlanarSurface, share boundaries is a geometric condition this type cannot
express; see diggs:SurfaceBoundedSolid.
