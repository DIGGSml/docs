Annotations
 
Abstract head for a load test applied through a bearing plate rather than directly
to an axially loaded element: diggs:PlateLoadTest (a single column head) and
diggs:ZoneLoadTest (a platform over a group of columns). It shares
diggs:AbstractLoadTestProcedureType (referenceSystem, maxTestLoad, increment) and
adds the plate (plateDiameter, plateWidth, plateLength, plateArea, plateStiffness),
the seating load, the level the plate bears at and the load-cycle procedure. It is
distinct from diggs:StaticLoadTest: ASTM D1143/D1143M and EN ISO 22477-1 measure the
axial capacity of a pile or column by loading it directly, whereas ASTM D1196, D1195
and DIN 18134 measure the stiffness of the ground or platform surface by loading it
through a plate. ASTM D1196 (nonrepetitive static plate load tests) is current; its
predecessor ASTM D1194 was withdrawn in 2003 but remains in wide use and is
registered. ASTM D1195 covers the repetitive variant. DIN 18134 prescribes exactly
two load cycles and reports two moduli, Ev1 (first loading) and Ev2 (second loading,
after initial compression), whose ratio Ev2/Ev1 is itself a reported
compaction-quality indicator. The enumeration of loadingProcedure spans all three
traditions: nonrepetitive, repetitive and two cycle. BS 8006-1 requires a zone load
test for reinforced-fill and rigid inclusion platform verification but does not
define the procedure, so a zone load test usually cites both it and the standard it
is executed to (D1196, D1195 or DIN 18134) through the repeatable inherited
testProcedureMethod. The moduli are results: give Ev1, Ev2 and the modulus of
subgrade reaction on the ResultSet of Test/outcome with the properties
deformation_modulus_first_load, deformation_modulus_second_load and
modulus_of_subgrade_reaction from properties.xml.
