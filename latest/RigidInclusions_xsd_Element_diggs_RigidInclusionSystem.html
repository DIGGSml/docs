Element diggs:RigidInclusionSystem
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
A rigid inclusion ground improvement system: the collection of load transfer
platforms and rigid inclusion columns whose information content is jointly
interpretable because they were built within one treated area. It is a group, not a
sampling feature: a system has an extent but no position, which is what the optional
extent of diggs:AbstractSamplingFeatureGroupType carries. The diggs:RITreatedArea
they were built within is the warrant for interpreting the columns and platforms
jointly. The treated area and the working platforms are properties of
diggs:RITreatedArea, not of this group. The extent of the group is not the treated
footprint, and the two should not be assumed equal: it is the area over which the
group acts or within which its members lie, and a system's members routinely include
exploratory boreholes drilled outside the improved footprint to characterize it, so
it is normally the larger. The treated footprint is the featureExtent of
diggs:RITreatedArea. Membership is declared by the members:
diggs:LoadTransferPlatform and diggs:RIColumn each cite this group through the
inherited diggs:samplingFeatureGroupRef, and the group holds no references to them.
