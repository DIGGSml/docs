Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
d_Ers (EBGEO) / D_e (FHWA): the diameter of the circular unit cell
of equal area to the tributary area that one inclusion supports,
derived from spacingX, spacingY and gridPattern. It is reported
explicitly because the unit-cell radius, not the raw
center-to-center spacing, enters most arching and settlement
formulations. It is a typed convenience element rather than a
dictionary parameter, because it is method-agnostic and computed
entirely from fields on this type. Where it disagrees with a
recomputation from spacingX, spacingY and gridPattern, the
recomputation governs.

Element diggs:DesignRIColumnType / diggs:areaReplacementRatio
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The ratio of inclusion (or cap) cross-sectional area to the
tributary area of one inclusion. It is an area-per-area measure, not
a dimensionless one: the quantity is quoted as a fraction about as
often as a percentage, and it is among the most-used numbers in
rigid inclusion design (coverage is typically under 10 percent
beneath embankments), so a file that reports 0.05 where a reader
expects 5 is a silent error. The explicit uom prevents it.

Element diggs:DesignRIColumnType / diggs:allowableMeanCompressiveStress
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
An absolute ceiling on mean compressive stress in the column,
applied on top of designCompressiveStrength rather than instead of
it. The two limits bind independently, and a design governed by the
absolute cap differs from one governed by the factored strength;
conflating them would lose which controlled. It is named generically
rather than after one practice's symbol, because several practices
reach the same constraint by different routes: a fixed cap in
European ground-improvement recommendations, and allowable-stress
tables for deep foundation materials in US building codes.

The axial load one column is designed to carry. It is a single
value, which is a simplification: the quantity varies by column
position (edge columns see different demand from interior ones) and
by load combination, and practice does not agree on which load to
use, from allowable bearing pressure times area, through sustained
service loads, to a conservative assumption that the columns take
the entire applied load. State the governing value here, and carry
the load case and basis as otherProperty on the planned column.

Element diggs:DesignRIColumnType / diggs:groutDesignRef

Annotations
 
The grout or concrete mix design or designs the columns are built
from. By basedOnRef resolution the most specific value wins, so a
design stating none uses the mix of the design it is based on, and a
zone-level design may state its own where a different mix applies.
It is a plain reference rather than basedOnRef, because a mix design
is a component of the work, not a generalization of it, and one mix
is normally shared across zones. The referenced diggs:DesignGroutMix
is declared as a sibling under the design slot of the program and
precedes this object. Given by reference only. Repeat for each mix.

          <annotation>
            <documentation>d_Ers (EBGEO) / D_e (FHWA): the diameter of the circular unit cell of equal area to the tributary area that one inclusion supports, derived from spacingX, spacingY and gridPattern. It is reported explicitly because the unit-cell radius, not the raw center-to-center spacing, enters most arching and settlement formulations. It is a typed convenience element rather than a dictionary parameter, because it is method-agnostic and computed entirely from fields on this type. Where it disagrees with a recomputation from spacingX, spacingY and gridPattern, the recomputation governs.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>

          </annotation>
        </element>
        <element minOccurs="0" name="columnDiameter" type="diggs:LengthMeasureType">
          <annotation>
            <documentation>The nominal designed shaft diameter of the columns. It is named to match diggs:RIColumnType/columnDiameter, the as-built counterpart, so that the design value and the constructed value carry one name and a conformance check has an unambiguous pair to compare.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>
        <element minOccurs="0" name="columnLength" type="diggs:LengthMeasureType">
          <annotation>
            <documentation>The designed column length, measured from cut-off to toe. Corresponds to diggs:RIColumnType/columnLength on the as-built column. Length and toeElevation are independent values; give whichever governs the design, or both.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>
        <element minOccurs="0" name="toeElevation" type="diggs:PointLocationPropertyType">
          <annotation>
            <documentation>The designed elevation of the column toe. It is a position, a one-dimensional position in a vertical CRS, as for every other elevation in DIGGS, not a scalar. It is the natural companion to the design bearing-stratum level: the toe elevation is normally set by the required penetration into that stratum, and the required penetration itself is an acceptance criterion, not a value here.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>
        <element minOccurs="0" maxOccurs="unbounded" name="bearingUnitRef" type="diggs:FeatureReferenceType">
          <annotation>
            <appinfo source="urn:x-gml:targetElement">diggs:GeoUnitObservation</appinfo>
            <documentation>The geotechnical unit the column is designed to bear in, as represented in the ground model used as the design basis. A design whose scope spans several models cites the bearing unit as it appears in each, and the one whose volume contains a given column applies to it; repeat for each model. Where a geoUnitDesignQuantity states a bearingStratumLevel, its geoUnitRef should be one of these units. It is distinct from diggs:RIColumnConstructionType/embedmentLayerRef, which records the unit the column actually terminated in. Given by reference only.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>
        <element minOccurs="0" name="columnHead" type="diggs:RIColumnHeadPropertyType">
          <annotation>
            <documentation>The head or cap this design requires at the top of the column, inline or by xlink:href to a diggs:RIColumnHead declared once and shared by every column designed to the same cap detail. It is the input to areaReplacementRatio: where a cap is present it is the cap plan area, not the shaft area, that is replaced, so a ratio stated without the cap dimension it was computed from is not reproducible. The punching-shear provisions consume headThickness the same way.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>
        <element minOccurs="0" name="areaReplacementRatio" type="diggs:AreaPerAreaMeasureType">
          <annotation>
            <documentation>The ratio of inclusion (or cap) cross-sectional area to the tributary area of one inclusion. It is an area-per-area measure, not a dimensionless one: the quantity is quoted as a fraction about as often as a percentage, and it is among the most-used numbers in rigid inclusion design (coverage is typically under 10 percent beneath embankments), so a file that reports 0.05 where a reader expects 5 is a silent error. The explicit uom prevents it.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>
        <element minOccurs="0" name="designCompressiveStrength" type="diggs:PressureMeasureType">
          <annotation>
            <documentation>The factored design compressive strength of the column material: f_cd in Eurocode terms, the characteristic strength reduced by a material factor, or the equivalent construction-method and inspection-level reduction other practices apply. It is distinct from allowableMeanCompressiveStress, and both may bind. The characteristic strength it is derived from is a property of the material, not of the column design, and belongs on the referenced diggs:DesignGroutMix.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>
        <element minOccurs="0" name="allowableMeanCompressiveStress" type="diggs:PressureMeasureType">
          <annotation>
            <documentation>An absolute ceiling on mean compressive stress in the column, applied on top of designCompressiveStrength rather than instead of it. The two limits bind independently, and a design governed by the absolute cap differs from one governed by the factored strength; conflating them would lose which controlled. It is named generically rather than after one practice's symbol, because several practices reach the same constraint by different routes: a fixed cap in European ground-improvement recommendations, and allowable-stress tables for deep foundation materials in US building codes.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>
        <element minOccurs="0" name="columnAxialCapacity" type="diggs:ForceMeasureType">
          <annotation>
            <documentation>The structural axial resistance of one column: its capacity as a structural member, governed by its material and cross section. It is not the geotechnical capacity, the sum of toe and shaft resistances, which is a per-soil-layer computation.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>
        <element minOccurs="0" name="designAxialLoad" type="diggs:ForceMeasureType">
          <annotation>
            <documentation>The axial load one column is designed to carry. It is a single value, which is a simplification: the quantity varies by column position (edge columns see different demand from interior ones) and by load combination, and practice does not agree on which load to use, from allowable bearing pressure times area, through sustained service loads, to a conservative assumption that the columns take the entire applied load. State the governing value here, and carry the load case and basis as otherProperty on the planned column.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>
        <element minOccurs="0" maxOccurs="unbounded" name="groutDesignRef" type="diggs:FeatureReferenceType">
          <annotation>
            <appinfo source="urn:x-gml:targetElement">diggs:DesignGroutMix</appinfo>
            <documentation>The grout or concrete mix design or designs the columns are built from. By basedOnRef resolution the most specific value wins, so a design stating none uses the mix of the design it is based on, and a zone-level design may state its own where a different mix applies. It is a plain reference rather than basedOnRef, because a mix design is a component of the work, not a generalization of it, and one mix is normally shared across zones. The referenced diggs:DesignGroutMix is declared as a sibling under the design slot of the program and precedes this object. Given by reference only. Repeat for each mix.</documentation>
          </annotation>
        </element>

A column typically passes through several units, each with its own relation per mechanism, so a multi-layer site states several diggs:LoadTransferRelation entries here.</documentation>
          </annotation>

It repeats per unit exactly as loadTransferRelation does; the populated fields of one entry vary with the role of the unit in the analysis (shaft-friction layer, bearing stratum, or the layer containing the neutral plane), as diggs:GeoUnitDesignQuantityType describes.</documentation>
