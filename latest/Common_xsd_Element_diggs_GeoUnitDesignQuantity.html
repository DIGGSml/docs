Annotations
 
The design-side numbers a deep-foundation axial analysis produces for one
geotechnical unit: negative skin friction limit, neutral-plane depth and peak axial
force, toe and shaft resistance, limit unit tip resistance, the adopted
bearing-stratum level and the subgrade reaction modulus, each keyed to the unit
through geoUnitRef, as for diggs:LoadTransferRelation. Generic design machinery, not
specific to rigid inclusions: neutral-plane analysis, end bearing and shaft
resistance are standard deep-foundation practice. One object serves every role, with
every field optional except geoUnitRef: shaft friction and shaft resistance are
stated once per layer the shaft passes through; toe resistance, limit tip resistance
and the bearing-stratum level apply only to the one unit that is the bearing
stratum; the neutral-plane depth and peak force describe a single neutral plane that
falls within exactly one unit; and the subgrade reaction modulus applies per
soft-soil layer under a load transfer platform. State the subset that applies to the
role of a unit: a shaft-friction layer states negativeSkinFrictionLimit and
shaftResistance and leaves the bearing-stratum fields absent, and the bearing
stratum states toeResistance, limitUnitTipResistance and bearingStratumLevel.
geoUnitRef is required because every number is meaningless without the unit it was
computed against; when the instance is the bearing-stratum instance it also supplies
the reference to the observed stratum top, so no second reference restates it.
limitState is carried once per object because the neutral-plane depth and peak force
are serviceability-limit-state quantities, and a file reporting them without the
limit state is ambiguous: negative skin friction should not be subtracted from
geotechnical resistance when computing the geotechnical factor of safety, and as the
factor of safety approaches 1 the neutral plane migrates to the top of the element.
