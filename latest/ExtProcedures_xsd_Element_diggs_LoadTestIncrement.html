Element diggs:LoadTestIncrement
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
One load application during a load test: a staged increment or decrement, a force
pulse or an impact. The readings taken during it are in incrementTimeSeries.
holdDuration and stabilizationCriterion are alternative hold criteria, not two ways
of recording the same fact: holdDuration (a fixed clock time) is how ASTM's Quick
Test and Constant Time Interval procedures define when to advance to the next
increment, and stabilizationCriterion (a rate of movement) is how EN ISO 22477-1's
maintained-load method and ASTM's Maintained Test define it. Give whichever the
governing procedure uses and leave the other absent.
