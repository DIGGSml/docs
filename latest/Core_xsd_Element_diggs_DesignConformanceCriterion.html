Element diggs:DesignConformanceCriterion
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
An acceptance criterion on a geometric, constructional or compositional property
measured against its design intent, such as position, verticality, diameter, length,
cut-off elevation, binding material content or a water/binding-material ratio. It is
a tolerance: it asks whether what was built matches what was designed, not whether
it performs. The parameterName of limit/AuthorizedParameter identifies the design
property; its mandatory codeSpace should resolve against designProperty.xml. The
schema does not enforce which dictionary is used. Where the requirement is on a
measured physical property, use diggs:PerformanceCriterion.
