Element diggs:RapidLoadTest
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
An axial compressive force-pulse (rapid) load test applied directly to a deep
foundation element, per ASTM D7383 or EN ISO 22477-10. The loading lasts tens to a
few hundred milliseconds, between the seconds-to-minutes timescale of
diggs:StaticLoadTest and the millisecond-or-shorter timescale of
diggs:DynamicLoadTest, and the measured force pulse needs an inertial and damping
correction (analysisMethod) to give a load-settlement response. EN ISO 22477-10
classifies a rapid test by purpose, as an investigation test (a sacrificial pile
loaded to the ultimate limit state) or a control test (a working pile loaded to a
specified load above the serviceability limit state); ASTM D7383 does not name the
distinction, so give testCategory where it applies and leave it out otherwise. The
two named analysis methods, the Unloading Point Method and its modified form, are
used by the practitioner communities of both standards.
