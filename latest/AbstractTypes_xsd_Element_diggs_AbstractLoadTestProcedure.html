Element diggs:AbstractLoadTestProcedure
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
Abstract head of the substitution group for load tests applied directly to a deep
foundation element or foundation system: static, rapid or dynamic. Its members are
diggs:StaticLoadTest, diggs:RapidLoadTest and diggs:DynamicLoadTest. It is not
specific to a sampling feature: a static load test on a driven pile, a drilled shaft
or a rigid inclusion is the same procedure. It is neutral between standards; cite
the standard or standards governing an instance, more than one if needed, in the
inherited testProcedureMethod, for example a rigid-inclusion test governed by ASIRI
and executed to EN ISO 22477-1. Shared by every load test: the reference system for
displacement (referenceSystem), the peak load (maxTestLoad), the peak stress
(maxTestStress), the weight of the loading assembly (deadLoad) and the load
increments applied (increment). Hold criteria differ between static, rapid and
dynamic tests, so increment is expected to be little used on the last two; see
diggs:RapidLoadTestType/pulseDuration and diggs:DynamicLoadTestType/hammerEnergy for
their single-event characterization. The reaction system has no field here: record
the reaction frame and jacks as equipment through the inherited
testProcedureEquipment (equipmentClass.xml includes load_test_reaction_frame and
hydraulic_jack). Readings taken during each increment are carried on the increment
(diggs:LoadTestIncrementType/incrementTimeSeries). Derived values such as the
load-settlement curve and the capacity belong on the results of the measurement that
cites the procedure.
