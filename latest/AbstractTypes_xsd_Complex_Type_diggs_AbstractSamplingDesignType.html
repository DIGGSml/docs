Element diggs:AbstractSamplingDesignType / diggs:activityType
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The kind of sampling activity planned: collect, aggregate, subsample
or test. Required, in parallel with
diggs:AbstractSamplingActivityType/activityType on the realized
side. Values come from the enumerated list.
Diagram
Type diggs:PlannedActivityTypeEnumType
Properties
content simple
Facets
enumeration collect 
The activity will create samples by collection from a sampling feature.
enumeration aggregate 
The activity will create samples by aggregating existing samples; the
sampleScope of the diggs:DesignSample cites more than one sourceSampleRef.
enumeration subsample 
The activity will create samples by subsampling an existing sample; the
sampleScope of the diggs:DesignSample cites exactly one sourceSampleRef.
enumeration test 
The activity will produce a test, standard or blank sample that does not
relate to any field sample or sampling feature.
