Element diggs:MeasurementSpecification
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
A program specification governing how a measurement is carried out, as distinct from
what its result must be: the method to follow, the quantities to record and the
equipment to use. It applies to any kind of measurement, such as an in-situ test, a
laboratory test or a monitoring campaign. Requirements the measurement must meet,
such as a minimum number of load increments, a maximum test stress or a hold time,
are criteria that name this specification through partOfSpecificationRef, and the
planned measurement cites the specification through programSpecificationRef.
Criteria on the measured result are performance criteria and are stated separately.
