Element diggs:ResistivityReadingType / diggs:nFactor
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The separation between the current dipole and the potential
dipole of a dipole-dipole array, as a multiple of
diggs:ResistivityReadingType/dipoleLength (the "n" in the
n-a notation) rather than as a length. The multiplier is
what a survey steps from one reading to the next at a fixed
dipole length.

Element diggs:ResistivityReadingType / diggs:orientation
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The bearing of the line along which the electrode array was laid
out, for surveys in which orientation is varied or otherwise
significant. An array line has no direction, so give the one of its
two opposed bearings that falls between 0 and 180 degrees. The
reference direction is carried by the refAzimuth attribute of the
measure.
