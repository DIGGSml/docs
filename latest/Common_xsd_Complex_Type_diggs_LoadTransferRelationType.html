Annotations
 
The geotechnical unit this relation applies to. Required, and per
layer. It references the diggs:GeoUnitObservation directly, the
per-unit object in the ground model, not the enclosing
diggs:GeoUnitSystem, whose anchoring choice states what the model
describes rather than which unit this relation applies to.

Element diggs:LoadTransferRelationType / diggs:curve
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The discretized (displacement, stress) series behind initialModulus
and limitValue, where the full curve shape matters and not only its
initial slope and plateau. It reuses diggs:ResultsPropertyType,
whose diggs:ResultSet holds parameters and a columnar dataValues
array: declare a displacement diggs:Property and a stress
diggs:Property under parameters, and the paired series under
dataValues. It carries no spatial domain, because the domain of a
t-z or q-z curve is displacement, not geometry.
