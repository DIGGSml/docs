Element diggs:AbstractProgramBasis
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
Abstract head of the substitution group for program bases: the objects a design was
prepared from, such as ground models, requirement sets, governing standards and
calculation inputs. A basis is evidence, not intent and not constraint: it is what
the designer relied on, and a design cites it through basisRef.
