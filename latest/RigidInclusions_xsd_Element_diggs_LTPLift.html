Element diggs:LTPLift
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
One lift of a load transfer platform: a discrete body of material laid down in a
single construction operation, with its own material and thickness. A lift is
defined by thickness, not by elevation, so it carries no elevation and no extent:
the plan extent is the featureExtent of the platform, which every lift shares.
Contrast diggs:Geosynthetic, a sheet at a level that states its position in its
extent geometry, then in its own elevation property, and only indirectly through
heightAboveWorkingPlatform. For a lift, liftThickness and heightAboveWorkingPlatform
are both mandatory and between them fix it completely: its thickness directly, and
its elevation as wpElevation plus heightAboveWorkingPlatform. Reached only through
diggs:LoadTransferPlatformType/lifts, which states the vertical invariant.
