Element diggs:DesignRIColumn
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The design of the rigid inclusion columns themselves: their layout, geometry,
material strength and axial capacity, for whatever population the inherited scope
declares: a site-wide population, one zone or a single column. It carries the
uniform values shared by every column in scope. Where a column differs from the
rest, plan it as its own diggs:DesignRIColumn, scoped to that column through
scope/targetPosition, with a basedOnRef to the design it departs from so that it
inherits everything it does not restate.
