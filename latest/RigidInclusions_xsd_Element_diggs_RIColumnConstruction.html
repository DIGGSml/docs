Element diggs:RIColumnConstruction
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The act of constructing one rigid inclusion column: drilling and, where the method
grouts through the auger stem, grouting. It carries the real-time telemetry record.
The method followed over each part of the column and the events recorded while it
was formed are stated through the constructionMethod and constructionEvent
properties inherited from diggs:AbstractConstructionActivity; where they disagree
with the record, the record governs. The inherited samplingFeatureRef must target a
diggs:RIColumn; XSD cannot say so, because the particle is declared on the abstract
base type and an extension cannot narrow it, so the reference-validation module
checks it. The activity is scoped to column construction only: its content model
(installationRecord, terminationOutcome, toeEmbedment, the embedment-layer choice,
riInstallationMethod, groutLevelCorrectiveAction) is meaningless for a platform.
Load transfer platform construction is compaction, not drilling or grouting, and has
no activity object, because nothing is routinely observed during LTP construction
and its results (lift structure, material, thickness) are recorded on the as-built
diggs:LoadTransferPlatform feature. A non-conformance cannot be carried by a
construction activity, since a feature may have none: diggs:NonConformance is
carried by diggs:AbstractSamplingFeatureType and diggs:AbstractSampleType.
