Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
How the movement measured during the test was referenced, for
example by an independent reference beam, optical survey or
satellite positioning. ASTM D1143 and EN ISO 22477-1 require the
reference to be independent of the reaction system and unaffected by
ground movement caused by the test; this element records the
arrangement used and does not assert that it met that requirement.
Values come from the enumerated list, or "Other: xx", where xx is a
user-defined term whose meaning differs from every listed value (not
a synonym of one).

Element diggs:AbstractLoadTestProcedureType / diggs:increment
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
A load increment applied during the test; repeat in sequence. A
static test recorded with a full loading schedule has several; a
rapid or dynamic test, a single pulse or blow, typically has none.
See diggs:LoadTestIncrementType for what each increment carries,
including the two alternative hold criteria: a fixed-duration hold
(as in the ASTM quick test) or a hold until the movement rate
stabilizes (as in the EN ISO 22477-1 maintained-load method).
