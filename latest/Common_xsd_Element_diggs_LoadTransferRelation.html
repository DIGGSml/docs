Element diggs:LoadTransferRelation
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
A shaft (t-z) or tip (q-z) load-transfer relation for one geotechnical unit: the
mobilization stiffness and limiting resistance that the shaft friction or tip
bearing of a column develops against that unit, together with which unit it applies
to and, optionally, the discretized curve behind the stated modulus. Generic design
machinery, not specific to rigid inclusions: t-z and q-z load-transfer analysis is
standard deep-foundation practice for driven piles, drilled shafts and micropiles.
An initial modulus alone discards the shape of the curve: k_tau and k_q are the
initial slopes of load-transfer curves, and in ASIRI practice they come from the
Frank and Zhao formulation, which is trilinear (an initial slope, a break, a reduced
slope and a plateau at the limiting value); the shape governs computed settlement,
so give the curve where it matters. A relation is per layer, since a single
site-wide k_tau is meaningless, and relationType and geoUnitRef are both required: a
classification with no layer, or a layer with no statement of which mechanism it
governs, is uninterpretable. Mind the sign convention when the object is used for
the negative skin friction limit q_s: above the neutral plane it is the downdrag
limit, the opposite sense from the positive shaft resistance below it, for the same
physical interface. State which regime a given relation describes in remark or in
the documentation of the referenced diggs:GeoUnitObservation, since limitValue alone
does not disambiguate it.
