Element diggs:RITreatedAreaType / diggs:seismicSiteClass
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The seismic site classification (IBC/ASCE 7 Site Class A-F) of the
site after ground improvement, based on the composite properties of
the treated ground mass. It is a classification, not the measurement
it is derived from: the composite shear-wave velocity is a separate
fact, expressible as a measured outcome of diggs:LabVelocityTest or
diggs:GeophysicalFieldSurvey, or as a diggs:PerformanceCriterion
where a minimum Vs is a design requirement. It is the achieved
class, not the intended one; its design-side counterpart is
diggs:DesignRITreatedAreaType/seismicSiteClass, which carries the
predicted class, and where the two differ that divergence is the
finding, so neither value should be edited to match the other.
Values come from the enumerated list, or "Other: xx", where xx is a
user-defined term whose meaning differs from every listed value (not
a synonym of one).
