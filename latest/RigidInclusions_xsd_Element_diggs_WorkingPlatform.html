Element diggs:WorkingPlatform
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
A surface prepared within the treated area before ground improvement begins,
designed to carry the load of the equipment used to construct the rigid inclusion
columns and load transfer platform(s). It is prepared to be horizontal and,
depending on site conditions, may be underlain by granular fill over the natural
ground surface to provide sufficient bearing capacity. If only one working platform
exists within the treated area, its wpExtent may be omitted and the extent is that
of diggs:RITreatedArea. Its wpElevation is mandatory and overrides any elevation in
wpExtent when wpExtent is in a 3D CRS. It is a component object, not a sampling
feature: it extends diggs:AbstractObjectType and is reached only through
diggs:RITreatedAreaType/workingPlatform, because it bounds the work rather than
being a thing the work is performed on; nothing is sampled from it and no
observation targets it. diggs:Geosynthetic is likewise a component of the
diggs:LoadTransferPlatform that contains it.
