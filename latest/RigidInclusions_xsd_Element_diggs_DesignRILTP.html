Element diggs:DesignRILTP
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The design of a load transfer platform: its thickness, the arching behavior it is
designed to develop, and the stresses that behavior delivers to the column heads and
to the soil between them. It is normally instance-scoped rather than
population-scoped, unlike diggs:DesignRIColumn: a site has a handful of platforms,
not thousands, so scoping one design to one platform through appliesToFeatureRef is
usually the right form.
