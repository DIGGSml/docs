Element diggs:RIColumnHeadType / diggs:headShape
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The plan shape of the head: square, rectangular, circular, hexagonal
or octagonal. It is needed to interpret headWidth, which is
otherwise ambiguous between the side of a square and the diameter of
a circle, which differ in area by 4/pi. It is not
diggs:PileShapeEnumExtType, the closest existing vocabulary, which
describes the structural cross section of a pile and has values
meaningless for a cap, such as "circular open" and "square hollow".
Values come from the enumerated list, or "Other: xx", where xx is a
user-defined term whose meaning differs from every listed value (not
a synonym of one).

Element diggs:RIColumnHeadType / diggs:headWidth
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
a: the governing plan dimension of the head: the side length of a
square cap, the diameter of a circular one, or the smaller side of a
rectangular one. It is the quantity called a in the arching
literature, and the one from which
diggs:DesignRIColumnType/areaReplacementRatio is computed where a
cap is present, since the cap rather than the shaft determines the
area replaced.

Element diggs:RIColumnHeadType / diggs:headLength
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The second plan dimension, for a rectangular head only. Omit it for
a square or circular head, where headWidth is sufficient. That
headShape and this element agree is a correlation the XSD cannot
express and is left to the validation chain.

Element diggs:RIColumnHeadType / diggs:headThickness
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The structural depth of the head, measured vertically.
Distinct from the plan dimensions above and consumed by a different
check: punching shear depends on the depth available to develop the
shear perimeter, not on the plan area.
