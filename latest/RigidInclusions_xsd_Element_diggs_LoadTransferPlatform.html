Element diggs:LoadTransferPlatform
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
A load transfer platform: the compacted granular layer, usually reinforced with
geosynthetics, constructed over a field of rigid inclusions to distribute structural
loads onto the column heads. It is a planar sampling feature, not an installation,
because it is constructed over the columns as a distinct layer rather than within
the rigid inclusion system. It cites the system it belongs to, as a statement of
membership, through the inherited optional diggs:samplingFeatureGroupRef. The rigid
inclusions beneath a platform are not enumerated here: each column cites its
platform through diggs:RIColumnType/ltpRef. Where ltpRef is absent, membership falls
back to testing the column's referencePoint against the featureExtent of the
platform, which is an inference rather than a statement.
