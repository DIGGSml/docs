Element diggs:DesignConstructionActivityType / diggs:samplingFeatureDesignRef
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The design of a feature the activity is to construct or act on; the
planned counterpart of diggs:samplingFeatureRef on the realized
activity. It cites a design, never a realized feature. Repeat: one
activity design may govern every feature a population design covers.
Given by reference only.
