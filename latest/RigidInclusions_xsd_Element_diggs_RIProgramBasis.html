Element diggs:RIProgramBasis
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The site-wide basis a rigid inclusion program's designs were prepared from: the
governing standards, design method, arching model, safety format, working life and
consequence class that make every other design value in the program interpretable. A
design cites it through basisRef, parallel to how diggs:DesignRITreatedArea is cited
through basedOnRef. It does not carry supportedStructureType, which is on
diggs:DesignRILTPType: what a platform supports is not one fact for the whole
program (a substation with nine differently loaded zones under one basis, say).
