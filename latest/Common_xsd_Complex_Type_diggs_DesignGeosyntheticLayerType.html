Element diggs:DesignGeosyntheticLayerType / diggs:membraneFactor
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
Ω: the membrane factor the beam method (FHWA GEC-13 Eq. 3-26,
tabulated against strain in Table 6-2, Young et al. 2003) multiplies
the load carried by this layer and half the design span by to obtain
designTension, where the more general parabolic method (Eq. 3-25)
instead solves for tension from tensileStiffness and atStrain
directly. It is a method-specific input to that output, not a
breakdown of it, and is specific to the FHWA beam method: BS 8006-1
(Eq 3-16) and EBGEO (Eq 3-20) obtain tension as tensileStiffness
times strain directly, and the ASIRI unit-cell method does not
compute geosynthetic membrane tension at all. It is a plain measure
type, not an authorized parameter, because its name alone identifies
it. Nothing in the schema links this value to the method that
produced it, and nothing prevents populating it alongside a design
that otherwise used BS 8006-1 or EBGEO; state the method in remark
or in the designMethod of the design's basis
(diggs:RIProgramBasisType, through basisRef), as for
diggs:DesignRILTPType/criticalHeight and the arching model.
