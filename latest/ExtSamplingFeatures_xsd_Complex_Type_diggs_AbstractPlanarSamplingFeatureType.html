Element diggs:AbstractPlanarSamplingFeatureType / diggs:featureExtent
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The extent of the surface feature, usable for visual representations
in simple mapping software. It is the only extent property needed:
the srsName of the surface may name either a spatial CRS or a
diggs:LocalCartesianCRS declared in DocumentInformation/crs, so a
feature described in local coordinates gives its extent here and
points srsName at that local CRS. relativeFeatureBoundary,
referenceEdge and planarReferencing are deprecated. The surface may
be given inline or cited with xlink:href, pointing at a surface
declared elsewhere in the document, so that one polygon need not be
restated everywhere it applies. A reference asserts identity: this
feature's extent is that surface, not an approximation of it or
derived from it. Where two extents differ, however slightly, each
states its own geometry and no reference is used. For example, an
as-built feature whose extent matched the designed extent it
realizes cites that surface, while one that departed from it carries
its own.

pits and excavations. Use one of its concrete extensions, for example
diggs:PlanarSamplingFeature.
