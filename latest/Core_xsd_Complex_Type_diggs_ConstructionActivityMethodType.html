Annotations
 
A specification of the method followed over the location, citing or
describing the standard, procedure or technique rather than only
naming it. Repeat for each specification that applies over the
location, for example a driving standard and a grouting standard.
State the equipment in the activity's equipment property.

Element diggs:ConstructionActivityMethodType / diggs:cuttingToolInfo
Namespace http://diggsml.org/schemas/3
Annotations
 
The cutting tools used by the method over the location, such as an
auger or a bit. The rig that drives the tool is the activity's
equipment; the tool is stated here because one rig works with
several tools and a method over part of a feature may use a
different tool from the rest.
