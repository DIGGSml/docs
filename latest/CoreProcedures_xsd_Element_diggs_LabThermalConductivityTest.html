Annotations
 
The procedure of a laboratory thermal needle probe test performed on a specimen
removed from a sampling feature (ASTM D5334, IEEE Std 442). A long, small-diameter
needle containing a heating element and a temperature sensor is inserted into the
specimen; a known current is passed through the heater and the resulting temperature
rise is monitored over time. Thermal conductivity and its reciprocal, thermal
resistivity, are computed from the rate of temperature rise per logarithmic time
cycle and are reported as diggs:Property values (propertyClass thermal_conductivity
or thermal_resistivity) in the outcome of the test, not as elements of this
procedure; one determination results per test. It is not for in-situ measurement:
the field-probe variant that ASTM D5334 and IEEE Std 442 also describe is out of
scope. Whether the specimen is undisturbed or remolded is recorded by
diggs:SoilSpecimenType/specimenType.
